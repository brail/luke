# Decisions in force — Documentation

> Current guidance, kept up to date; not a decision record. The ADRs linked here decide: where this page and an Accepted ADR disagree, the ADR holds.

_Last reviewed: 2026-11-22_

## Rules in force

- English is the sole canonical language for mutable technical documentation, repository instructions, source comments, developer-facing diagnostics and logs, and other technical prose. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md), [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- Italian is permitted only in genuine product UI and end-user interaction, temporarily, pending a separate internationalization cycle; a string's audience decides the exception, not its location, and developer-facing text never inherits it. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- Local consistency never overrides the audience rule: developer-facing prose is not kept in Italian to match the file around it. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md), [ADR-015](../015-documentation-architecture-and-canonical-language.md)
- Any future Italian documentation is generated from a canonical English source with a way to detect drift from it, never maintained as an equal source, and no layout, marker, translation system or pipeline is prescribed before a real derived translation exists. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md), [ADR-015](../015-documentation-architecture-and-canonical-language.md)
- The canonical-language decision may be reopened only by a later superseding ADR the owner explicitly authorizes and accepts; a review finding, audit appendix, checker change or standalone instruction-file edit is evidence, never a supersession. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md), [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- The repository README is the single navigation root for governed Markdown, every governed document is reachable from it through relative links and maintained indexes, and documentation stays beside the code that owns it where that helps maintenance. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- Reader-facing navigation is organized by purpose, while a document's authority and mutability decide how it may change. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- Mechanical properties (link resolution, reachability, index integrity, required workspace documentation, the canonical language) are enforced deterministically to the extent a check can decide them; accuracy, completeness and documentation impact stay with evidence-based human or agent review. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- Documentation impact is declared with evidence at code-change handoff, in the operational form the repository instruction file states ([`CLAUDE.md`, "Canonical Language and Documentation Impact"](../../../CLAUDE.md#canonical-language-and-documentation-impact)). [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- Historical records keep their append-only or immutable lifecycle but are English: an Italian body is translated without changing its status, findings or chronology, or retired from the tree, the owner choosing per document, and anything appended afterwards is English. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- A retired record stays traceable: the index that listed it keeps an English entry with its last path and the commit that holds it, current documents that linked it point to that entry, and documents that only cite it, frozen ledgers included, are not rewritten. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- An Accepted ADR's Context, Decision and Consequences are never edited, and its Status changes only to record a supersession or deprecation the owner authorized. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- A statement of fact about the repository in an Accepted ADR (a path, file, rule, procedure or route name, count, observed gap) that a later change made untrue is corrected by a dated English entry in a final `## Errata` section that locates or quotes it, says what is true now and cites the change or record that made it so. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- An erratum corrects a fact and nothing else: a correction that would change what an ADR decides, requires or rejects needs a superseding ADR, an unclear case is superseded, reviewers refuse an erratum that changes a requirement, and tooling may report candidate errata but never writes them. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md)
- New errata sections cite ADR-047, and existing ones stay as written. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- The one-time translation of the records older than ADR-015 is complete, and its exception extends to no later record. [ADR-030](../030-documentation-architecture-canonical-language-and-historical-records.md), [ADR-015](../015-documentation-architecture-and-canonical-language.md)
- Every record belongs to one area, the areas are the pages in `docs/decisions/in-force/`, adding, merging or renaming one is an ordinary documentation change, and a superseded record takes the area of the record at the head of its chain. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- Each area page states the current rules in short bullets, each linking the record that decides it and naming the operations it covers and what it excepts, and lists the area's records in force, the superseded records whose text one in force still keeps, and related records of other areas. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- A page links, never copies, a list its record leaves to another maintained document, and a rule appears on a page only with a record behind it. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- A page decides nothing: where it and an Accepted record disagree, the record holds and the page is corrected, and the page is updated in the commit that adds a record or an erratum correcting a fact it states. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- `docs/decisions/README.md` must list every record once, under its area, records in force first and the others folded below, generated from the record files and the pages and compared with them by the documentation check. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- A new record is one or two pages and states only what changes, the rule it adds or what it replaces in the record it supersedes and links, without restating what stands; supersession stays whole, and errata stay for facts. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- Records older than ADR-047 keep their length, and a long chain kept by reference is shortened only by a new record the owner asks for, never by editing. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- At each major release, and at least once a year, records and pages are reviewed (statuses and kept clauses against the code, each page against its records and their errata, chains kept by reference, citations of superseded records), and each review must be dated on the pages and listed in the index. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
- Review findings become errata, page corrections or proposals for a new record, and the owner decides every status change and every new record. [ADR-047](../047-documentation-architecture-decisions-in-force-index-by-area-and-short-records.md)
