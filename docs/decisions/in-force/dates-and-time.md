# Decisions in force — Dates and time

> Current guidance, kept up to date; not a decision record. The ADRs linked here decide: where this page and an Accepted ADR disagree, the ADR holds.

_Last reviewed: 2026-11-22_

## Rules in force

- Luke has four date primitives: the calendar day (`CalendarDate`, `YYYY-MM-DD`, `CalendarDateSchema`, stored as `@db.Date` or as a UTC-midnight timestamp with `allDay`, an encoding and not an instant), the instant (`Date`, ISO 8601 in UTC, `timestamp(3)`, `CalendarInstantSchema`), the wall time (`HH:mm`, `WallTimeSchema`) and the time zone (an IANA name, `TimeZoneSchema`). [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md#errata), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md)
- Every date schema and helper lives in `@luke/core`: `schemas/dates.ts`, `utils/dateUtils.ts`, and the domain rules beside them (`utils/calendarEventLock.ts` for deadlines and locks). [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- A calendar day becomes an instant only by naming the zone and the wall time (`startOfDayIn`, `instantAt`), an instant becomes a day only by naming the zone (`calendarDateIn`), and a day's UTC-midnight encoding is never read as a moment. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- The business zone, `app.defaultTimezone`, decides deadlines, lateness, working days, locks, deadline notices, the dashboard's weekly sales, calendar cloning, template application and the company's scheduler slots (the daily backup). [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- The reader zone — the profile zone the server resolves (`getUserTimeZone`, falling back to the business zone) and `me.get` returns — decides everything else a person reads or types: web views and inputs, exports, emails, and digests, which are also sent at an hour of the recipient's zone. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- The browser zone never reads an instant for a signed-in person: it only proposes a profile update (`TimezoneUpdateDialog`) and dates instants, naming the zone, for a reader without a session; the clocks widget's zones are named explicitly, validated by `dashboard.saveConfig` with `TimeZoneSchema`, and belong to neither role. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- Two zones are the same when `Intl` resolves them to the same name in the same runtime (`sameTimeZone`), not when their strings are equal; `groupByTimeZone` still groups digest and maintenance cohorts by spelling, an open gap. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- Shifting by N days has one contract, `shiftEventByDays` in core: an all-day event moves both of its days by calendar arithmetic, and a day-aligned timed event (both ends at the start of their day in the named zone, the end after the start) moves each end to the start of the day N days later, a date the zone skipped altogether starting when the next one does. [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md)
- Any other timed event keeps the wall time of each end, to the millisecond: a wall time the target day skips is read with the offset before the gap, and a repeated one keeps its occurrence where it exists, else the first. [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md)
- The moved end is kept only where it still comes after the moved start, as the original did, and otherwise follows it at the original duration; a zero-length interval stays zero, an inverted one stays inverted for the caller to refuse, N = 0 is the identity, and adding `N × 86 400 000` ms is not a calendar shift. [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md)
- Calendar cloning (`cloneFromBrandSeason`) moves every event with `shiftEventByDays` in the business zone, and a calendar drag or Gantt resize moves an event with it in the profile zone. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md#errata), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- A form rebuilds an instant from a day and a wall time only when the user changed them; saving an unchanged value sends back the stored instant. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- A time typed in a form is resolved by `instantAt`, a skipped wall time becoming the first instant after the gap and a repeated one its first occurrence; every core helper that resolves a wall time states how it treats both, and callers do not choose their own. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- Calendar days run from 1900-01-01 to 9999-12-30; instants have a valid calendar day as their UTC date, `[1900-01-01T00:00Z, 9999-12-31T00:00Z)`; timed instants a person enters lie in `[1900-01-02T00:00Z, 9999-12-30T00:00Z)`. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- For events the timed bound is checked where their dates are written, by `assertEventDates` on the effective `allDay` — create, reschedule, every update that changes them, template application and cloning — and any other timed input carries it in its schema (`TimedInstantSchema`); a result out of range is refused with a message, never a `RangeError`. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md#errata)
- `seasonCalendar.applyTemplate` takes its anchor as a calendar day and writes a timed item from the start of its first day to the start of the day after its last, in the business zone; `deadlineDay` and `eventCalendarDays` read a timed end as exclusive. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md#errata), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md)
- Database CHECK constraints hold `calendar_events` (by `allDay`), `planning_groups`, `holidays` and `vendor_closure_periods` to the same bounds; dates no constraint covers, inside audit metadata or from an external source, are validated where they are read or imported, and a reader degrades one malformed value instead of failing the whole result. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- `holidays.previewImport` and `holidays.confirmImport` read Nager.Date's answer through `NagerHolidaysSchema` before anything is written, and the import is all or nothing, in one transaction. [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- NAV's blank date, `1753-01-01`, is a missing value: the season sync stores `Starting Date` and `Ending Date` through `navDateOrNull` as `null`, while the `nav_pf_*` replica columns still store the blank date. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- The web hook that provides the reader zone exposes whether it is ready: until it is, labels show a placeholder and conversions, form initialisation, submissions and client-side exports wait, and an error is reported, never replaced by another zone. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- A form captures the zone when it opens, names it next to its time fields and converts with it, and code outside React receives the zone and the locale as arguments. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md)
- Instants are formatted only by core, with an explicit zone (`formatInstant`), and calendar days without one (`formatCalendarDate`); an invalid zone is an error, and until the i18n cycle every date uses the core default locale, `it-IT`. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- The audit-log export writes instants in UTC and says so, and other exports write them in the reader zone and name it, except the season-calendar PDF and XLSX, which do not name it yet. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- A new user takes `app.defaultTimezone` when created (`users.create`, the first LDAP login, the seed), and `User.timezone` has no database default; stored zones are left as they are. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
- `@luke/no-implicit-zone-date` refuses, as an error, the zone-implicit forms ADR-035 §Enforcement lists, in the TypeScript sources of the apps and packages, the API's scripts and seeds, `tools/` and `scripts/`; tests and core's `utils/dateUtils.ts` and `schemas/dates.ts` are outside it, and each exemption in source carries its reason. [ADR-035](../035-date-and-time-model-calendar-days-instants-and-two-zone-roles.md), [ADR-036](../036-date-and-time-model-moving-both-ends-of-a-timed-event.md#errata)
